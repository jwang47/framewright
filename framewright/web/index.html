Film

Roughness clumps the grain; colour grain gives each dye layer its own. Halation is the warm glow film gets round bright highlights; bloom is the same glow, neutral.

Masks M

finding edges…

Drag the dots on the photo. Radial and rect: Shift keeps it round or square; drag a rect's top dot to turn it (Shift snaps to 15°). Falloff fades a rect from its top dot's end to the far end, like a beam of light. Range limits the mask to dark (low) or bright (high) areas. Subject: brush roughly over it, spill and all, and the mask snaps to its edges; Erase (or ⌥) marks what isn't it.

Slide

H/L or arrows switch slides. Drag in a photo to reposition it; drag it onto another tile to swap them, or drag a photo from below the slides onto a tile. Drag the line between the big tile and the rest to resize. Pinch or ctrl-scroll to zoom. Click the selected tile again, the gap, or Esc to deselect. Fill → Square → Whole: Square shows a square crop in the middle of the tile, Whole the full photo, both with the background around them.
